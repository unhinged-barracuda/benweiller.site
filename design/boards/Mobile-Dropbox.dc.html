today our life stories are written digitally, which means we’re stuck lugging around our digital baggage. enter Dropbox.

/a peek inside the box

100 days of featured memories, boxed but not forgotten.

file 038: photos from your old but still promising hand modeling portfolio.

file 139: all those clips you downloaded during your brief obsession with cats singing.

file 008: the schematics from your first attempt at building a time machine, and your 100th attempt.

/the museum of digital baggage

a physical museum for digital memories, The Dropbox Museum of Digital Baggage proudly displays some of the world’s wildest digital oddities.

/tidying up

it’s hard to reminisce when the inside of your account looks like the floor of a messy closet. tutorial videos by organization experts, The Home Edit, show you how to organize and prioritize your digital baggage.

/behind the baggage

tune in to this weekly podcast to unpack some of the crazy stories and wacky tales that live behind the digital baggage people store in their Dropbox accounts.
