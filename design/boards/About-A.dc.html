who, me?

as a kid you might find me binging Avatar: The Last Airbender on Nickelodeon, buried in a good book or coming up with siiiick new tricks on my old school Razor scooter. “The Illustrated Man” by Ray Bradbury along with a litany of other literature inspired me to pursue my passion for wordsmithing and storytelling. later, at the University of Denver, I was enthralled by the world of big ideas and entrepreneurship. before long my two interests merged and I found myself sitting in class at Denver Ad School.

I’m infinitely passionate about crafting new ideas and weaving syllables into sentences, which you’ll find me doing on the daily. you can also catch me writing poetry, short fiction, cycling, camping and traveling the world. group rides and group writes encouraged.
